Spirometry Curve Analyzer

Flow–time and volume–time measurements, processed locally in your browser.

For research and education only. This application does not validate maneuver acceptability or repeatability, or supply clinically validated reference equations, diagnoses, or severity grades. Do not use it for patient care.

1. Choose a waveform

Requires a time column and a flow or volume column (e.g. time_s,flow_Lps or time_s,volume_L). Up to 20,000 samples / 2 MB. Millisecond and millilitre units are recognized when present in headers.

Synthetic demonstration

Privacy: This page reads your file locally. It makes no requests to a server for measurements and does not store your inputs or use analytics.

3. Expiratory flow–volume plot

Only the recorded expiratory limb is shown. No inspiratory curve is inferred.
